## Keap1_Nrf2_45peptide_dataset.csv

One row per peptide, 45 rows.

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| `peptide_id`, `round`, `length_aa`, `sequence` | identity and design round |
| `nrf2_77_84_aligned`, `pos79`, `pos80`, `pos81`, `pos82` | alignment to the Nrf2 recognition positions |
| `haddock_score`, `dockq`, `fnat`, `i_rmsd_A`, `l_rmsd_A`, `il_rmsd_A`, `rmsd_A`, `bsa_A2`, `edesolv`, `eelec`, `evdw` | HADDOCK3 metrics for the representative model |
| `chai_iptm_mean`, `chai_ptm_mean`, `chai_receptor_peptide_iptm_mean`, `chai_aggregate_mean`, `chai_n_models` | Chai-1 confidence, averaged over the five predicted models |
| `inhibition_1uM_pct`, `inhibition_10uM_pct`, `active_gt50pct_1uM` | fluorescence-polarization competition assay |

`round` distinguishes the 28 designs selected under criteria fixed before any
experimental data were obtained (`first-round`) from the 16 analogues designed
after that result was known (`focused-redesign`) and the native Nrf2 12-mer
(`reference`). HADDOCK3 metrics and Chai-1 confidence are present for the 28
first-round designs only.

Inhibition values are means of duplicate measurements from single experiments,
reported without dispersion estimates; all peptides were measured in the same
assay format using a single recombinant Keap1 lot. Strongly inhibiting peptides
are saturated at both screening concentrations. See the Scope and Limitations
section of the paper before using these values quantitatively.

## Position numbering

Residue numbers refer to full-length human Nrf2. Every design template held the
C-terminal Leu84 fixed, so for a peptide of length *L* the residue corresponding
to Nrf2 position *r* occupies index *r* − 84 + *L* (1-based). The native residues
at the recognition positions are Asp77, Glu78, Glu79, Thr80, Gly81, Glu82, Phe83.

## Notes on the counts

Stepwise counts were re-derived from the sequence files here and match the
values used in the paper, 5,118 in total.

The HADDOCK3 checkpoint here covers the six length classes of 10–15 residues,
115 sequences.

The Chai-1 predictions and the structure-based selection made from them are in
the archival record rather than here, because of their size. Reapplying the
filter to them returns the Chai-1 column of Table 1 for those classes: 71, 51,
32, 41, 43 and 43 models, 281 in total.
